# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, collaborators, and hiring partners evaluating Minhaj Gouda's leadership, operations, and live-production experience.

## Product Purpose

Present Minhaj's work and operating experience with enough clarity and confidence for a visitor to understand his scale, judgment, and fit, then make contact.

## Brand Personality

Confident, kinetic, composed. The experience should feel capable under pressure, with energy that supports the story instead of competing with it.

## Anti-references

Avoid a photo-led hero, noisy or glitch-heavy effects, generic startup-template patterns, and animation that feels ornamental or makes the page harder to read.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the operating point of view, not decoration.
- Make scale and responsibility feel tangible through composition and pacing.
- Keep the interface composed while letting motion carry energy.
- Use direct language and visible proof of delivery.
- Let every interaction preserve the visitor's path toward the work and contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with a static, fully usable fallback. Motion must not carry essential meaning, and interactive elements must remain keyboard accessible with visible focus states.
